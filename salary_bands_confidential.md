# Salary Bands (Confidential - HR only) - Nexora Technologies

## Salary Bands
Level L1 pays Rs. 4 to 7 lakh per annum. Level L2 pays Rs. 7 to 12 lakh per annum. Level L3 pays Rs. 12 to 20 lakh per annum. Level L4 pays Rs. 20 to 35 lakh per annum.

## Variable Pay
Variable pay is 10 percent of fixed pay for levels L1 and L2, and 15 percent of fixed pay for levels L3 and L4.

## Increment Guidelines
A rating of 5 earns an increment of 12 to 15 percent. A rating of 4 earns 8 to 10 percent. A rating of 3 earns 5 to 7 percent. A rating of 2 or below earns no increment.
