# Leave Policy - Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## Casual Leave
Every employee gets 12 casual leaves per calendar year. Casual leave can be taken in half-day or full-day units. It cannot be carried forward to the next year. A maximum of 3 casual leaves can be taken consecutively.

## Sick Leave
Employees get 10 sick leaves per year. If sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate from a registered doctor must be submitted to HR within 3 days of returning to work.

## Earned Leave (Privilege Leave)
Earned leave accrues at 1.5 days per month (18 days per year). Unused earned leave can be carried forward up to a maximum balance of 30 days. Earned leave must be applied for at least 7 days in advance, except in emergencies. Employees on probation can take earned leave only after confirmation.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees are entitled to 10 days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

## Public Holidays
The company provides 12 public holidays and 2 optional holidays per year. The holiday list is published every January on the HR portal.

## Applying for Leave
All leave must be applied through the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager. Unapproved absence of more than 3 consecutive days may be treated as absconding and can lead to disciplinary action. Leave without pay (LWP) is deducted from monthly salary on a per-day basis.
