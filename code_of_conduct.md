# Code of Conduct - Nexora Technologies

## Professional Behaviour
Employees must treat colleagues, clients and vendors with respect. Discrimination based on gender, religion, caste, disability or any other personal characteristic is strictly prohibited.

## Dress Code
Business casual is followed on office days. Formal attire is required during client visits. Casual wear is allowed on Fridays.

## Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)
Nexora has zero tolerance for sexual harassment. Complaints can be raised with the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) at icc@nexora.example. All complaints are handled confidentially, and retaliation against a complainant is a punishable offence.

## Confidentiality
Employees must not share confidential company, client or employee information with outsiders, during or after employment. Violation can lead to termination and legal action.

## Conflict of Interest
Employees must declare any side business, freelance work or investments that may conflict with their role. Working for a competitor while employed at Nexora is not allowed.

## Gifts
Gifts from clients or vendors worth more than Rs. 2,000 must be declared to HR and may need to be returned.

## Disciplinary Process
Violations lead to a verbal warning, written warning, suspension or termination depending on severity. Employees get a chance to present their side before any action is taken.
