# Payroll, Benefits and Exit Policy - Nexora Technologies

## Salary Payment
Salary is credited on the last working day of every month. Payslips are available on the HR portal by the 3rd of the next month.

## Provident Fund
Both employee and employer contribute 12 percent of basic salary to the Provident Fund (PF).

## Health Insurance
Every employee gets group health insurance coverage of Rs. 5 lakh per year, which also covers spouse and up to 2 children. Parents can be added at an additional cost paid by the employee.

## Reimbursements
Eligible expenses such as travel and client meetings must be claimed within 30 days of the expense with valid bills. Claims are paid with the next month's salary after manager approval.

## Probation and Confirmation
The probation period is 3 months. Confirmation is based on the manager's performance review. Probation can be extended by up to 3 months.

## Performance Reviews and Increments
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in April and October. Salary increments are applied from April based on the annual rating.

## Resignation and Notice Period
The notice period is 60 days for confirmed employees and 30 days for employees on probation. Employees can request a buyout of the notice period with HR and management approval. Final settlement is completed within 45 days of the last working day. Experience letter and relieving letter are issued after clearance of all dues and company assets.
