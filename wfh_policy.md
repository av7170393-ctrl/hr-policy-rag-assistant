# Work From Home (Hybrid) Policy - Nexora Technologies

## Hybrid Model
Nexora follows a hybrid work model. Employees work 3 days from the office and 2 days from home each week. Office days are Monday, Tuesday and Thursday by default. Teams may agree on different office days with manager approval.

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 3-month probation are eligible for the hybrid model. Employees on probation must work from the office 5 days a week unless the manager approves otherwise.

## Core Hours
Irrespective of location, all employees must be available between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM on working days for meetings and collaboration.

## Extra WFH Requests
Additional WFH days beyond the standard 2 days per week need written approval from the reporting manager. A maximum of 10 extra WFH days per year can be approved. Full remote work for more than 2 weeks continuously requires HR approval.

## Internet and Equipment Allowance
Employees get a monthly internet allowance of Rs. 1,000 for WFH days. The company provides a laptop; employees are responsible for keeping company equipment safe. A VPN must be used when accessing company systems from home.

## Data Security at Home
Company data must not be stored on personal devices. Screens must be locked when away. Confidential calls should not be taken in public places.
