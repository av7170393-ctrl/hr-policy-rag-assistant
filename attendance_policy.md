# Attendance Policy - Nexora Technologies

## Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, including a 1-hour lunch break. Total expected working time is 9 hours per day (8 hours of work plus 1 hour break).

## Grace Period
Employees get a grace period of 15 minutes for late arrival, up to 3 times per month. From the 4th late arrival onwards, half a day of casual leave is deducted for every 3 late marks.

## Half Day and Absence
Working fewer than 4.5 hours in a day is marked as an absence for that day. A half day is counted when an employee works between 4.5 and 8 hours.

## Marking Attendance
Attendance is marked through the biometric system in office and through the HR portal check-in on WFH days. Missed punches must be regularized in the HR portal within 3 working days.

## Overtime and Weekend Work
Overtime is not paid by default. If an employee is asked to work on a weekend or public holiday, they get a compensatory off, which must be used within 30 days.
