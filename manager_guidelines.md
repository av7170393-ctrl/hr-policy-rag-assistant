# Manager Guidelines - Nexora Technologies

## Leave Approval
Managers must approve or reject leave requests within 2 working days. A rejected request must include a written reason shared with the employee.

## Expense Approval Limits
Managers can approve expense claims up to Rs. 25,000 per claim. Claims above this limit need approval from the department head.

## Performance Ratings
Performance is rated on a 5-point scale. A maximum of 20 percent of a team can receive the top rating of 5.

## Disciplinary Action
A manager must consult HR before issuing any written warning. Termination requires approval from both HR and the department head.
